The real problems

8 reasons most med spa websites lose treatment researchers before consultation

No transparent pricing on common treatments

Botox per unit, laser hair removal per area, lip filler per syringe — prospects shop these. Sites hiding prices behind Contact us lose to spas publishing starting-at ranges.

Generic Treatments page where dedicated treatment pages should live

Botox, fillers, laser, microneedling, IPL, body contouring — each is a separate search intent. One Treatments page collapses all of it and ranks for none.

Provider credentials hidden or missing

MD, DO, NP, PA, RN, board certifications, training (Galderma, Allergan Medical Institute, Aesthetic Mastery Program) — trust signals med spa prospects actively look for.

No before-and-after gallery with proper disclosure

Med spa is sold on visual results. Before-and-after photos with proper consent and FTC-compliant individual-results-may-vary language win the visual-decision prospect.

No FDA-cleared device list

BroadBand Light, Morpheus8, Sciton, CoolSculpting, Ulthera, EmSculpt — FDA-cleared device branding is a trust signal and a search term in itself.

Generic About page where physician-led model should live

Med spas under physician medical direction win the credentialed-prospect segment. Sites that bury the medical director or omit medical direction entirely lose this segment.

No financing partnership shown

Average med spa package in 2026 hit $1,840. Without CareCredit, Cherry, PatientFi, or Alphaeon Credit visible, leveraged-buy prospects book elsewhere.

Online booking flow is a generic iframe

Booking trapped in a 600px iframe from Boulevard, Aesthetic Record, or Symplast breaks mobile and dilutes branding. Native API integration changes conversion.

8 features med spa owners actually use (and most websites skip)

Every feature on this list exists because we've seen it move the needle on booked leads. Nothing here is decoration.

Per-treatment landing pages with proper FDA-cleared device branding

Botox, Dysport, Restylane, Juvederm, Sculptra, Morpheus8, BroadBand Light — each treatment gets its own page with mechanism, expectations, downtime, FTC-compliant outcomes.

Transparent starting-at pricing

Per-unit Botox, per-area laser, per-syringe filler, per-treatment IPL — starting prices visible by treatment. Filters tire-kickers and qualifies serious consultations.

Provider profile pages with credentials

MD, DO, NP, PA, RN credentials, board certifications, training programs, years of experience, treatment specialties. Books-by-provider rates jump 2–3x when prospects can pick.

Before-and-after gallery with FTC compliance

Real patient photos with consent, individual-results-may-vary notation, treatment details. Schema-marked for search visibility.

Financing with monthly pricing

CareCredit, Cherry, PatientFi, Alphaeon Credit — financing visible on every treatment page with as-low-as messaging for leveraged buyers.

Membership program signup

Tiered membership (Beauty Club, VIP, Platinum) with monthly credit, member-only event pricing, treatment-of-the-month features. Drives recurring revenue.

Native online booking, not a Boulevard iframe

Aesthetic Record, Boulevard, Symplast, Nextech integrated via API where possible. Booking feels native to your site, not an embedded foreign object.

Consultation booking with intake form

Treatment interest, skin concerns, medical history disclosure (HIPAA-aware), and provider preference captured upfront. First consultation is real work, not tell-me-about-yourself.

What we won't promise

Page-one rankings for competitive searches like "Botox [city]" take time. We focus on realistic treatment, neighborhood, and local-intent keywords that can build visibility steadily.

Beating National Platforms Overnight

RealSelf, Allergan, and major aesthetic platforms have years of authority behind them. We compete where it matters most—local searches, treatment-specific queries, and Google Maps visibility.

Frequently asked questions

Is the $999 price really all-in?

Yes. Logo, branding, salon or spa website, SEO content, on-page SEO setup, Google Business Profile optimization, and 30 days of post-launch support. No hidden fees. Booking platform subscription (Vagaro, Booksy, Square) is separate — that's your existing tool, not something we sell.

Will my site rank #1 for 'Botox [my city]'?

While no ethical SEO provider can guarantee the #1 spot overnight, we optimize your site's technical structure, local citations, and on-page metadata to target high-intent local search queries like Botox in your city effectively.

How do you handle FDA and state medical board compliance?

We structure your site layout and content with compliance best practices in mind, ensuring proper medical disclosures, disclaimers, and practitioner information are appropriately displayed according to standard guidelines.

Do you integrate with Aesthetic Record, Boulevard, Zenoti, or Nextech?

Yes, we seamlessly integrate booking widgets and client management links from your preferred industry platforms directly into your new website design.

What if I already have a site on Wix, Squarespace, or an old WordPress build?

We handle migration smoothly, ensuring your content is upgraded, URLs are redirected properly to protect your existing search rankings, and your new site launches without a hitch.

Do you do ongoing SEO or content writing after launch?

While the package includes comprehensive initial on-page setup and optimization, we also offer flexible ongoing growth and content packages if you want to continuously expand your reach.

How do you handle physician oversight and medical director display?

We ensure your medical director's credentials, name, and required regulatory info are clearly highlighted in your footer and team/about sections as mandated by local laws.

Can you build a multi-location med spa site?

Yes, we can architect your site with dedicated location pages and local optimization tailored for each clinic branch you operate.

What happens after the 30-day support window?

After your 30 days of post-launch support, your site is entirely yours to manage, or you can opt into one of our maintenance plans for ongoing peace of mind and updates.

How does this compare to RealSelf or Allergan-network paid promotion?

Unlike rented directory space or temporary ad networks, your custom website is a permanent asset that you completely own, building long-term brand equity and organic leads without recurring ad overhead.
